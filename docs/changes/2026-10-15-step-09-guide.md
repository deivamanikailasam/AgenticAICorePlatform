# Explain sandboxed code execution for learners

**Step:** 09 · **Commit:** docs(guide): add step 09 chapter and ADR 0019 · **Date:** 2026-10-15

## What changed
The Step 9 chapter explains gVisor, the hardened container profile, the two clocks and the
tar-in/tar-out design, with the measured outcomes of the hostile tests. ADR 0019 records the
engine choices and an open question: Firecracker and the managed sandbox are stubs because this
build has no KVM and no vendor. `PROGRESS.md` lists that as a decision for the owner.

## Why
Running untrusted code is the highest-risk tool; its rules must be clear and its limits honest.

## How to check it
Open `docs/guide/step-09-sandboxed-code-execution.md` and `docs/adr/0019-code-sandbox-engines.md`.

## Risks and follow-ups
Owner decision on the stub engines.
