# Overlays and the agent-project template pinned to the images of 1887123

## What changed
- dev, staging and prod now pin the images that the images workflow pushed, signed and scanned
  for 1887123 (images run 39004313391). Until now they pinned 1ba0391.
- `just template-data` regenerated the template from these pins: `platform_ref` is now 1887123.

## Why
The images of 1ba0391 don't contain the Step 40 code: the knowledge assistant's harness, the
`publish_report` tool, and nested specs running as their own agents under the parent's
controls. research-report 0.2.0, now a prod canary candidate, needs all three.

## How to check it
- `just deploy-check`: 0 problems.
- `just template-data-check`: up to date.
- deploy-e2e and template-e2e run on this commit.

## Risks and follow-ups
Prod's overlay is a governance path: on main this bump needs the named approver.
