# Explain Step 1 for learners, and start the setup and architecture guides

**Step:** 01 · **Commit:** docs(guide): add step 01 chapter, setup guides, architecture and glossary · **Date:** 2026-10-15

## What changed
The Step 1 chapter follows the 13-section template: what we built, why, how, alternatives,
mistakes and a "Done when" table that separates what was verified here from what still needs
the Mac. `docs/setup/local.md` walks a teammate through setup on the Mac with a troubleshooting
table; `docs/setup/production.md` records the promotion principles fixed so far.
`docs/architecture/README.md` redraws the spec's two diagram widgets as Mermaid, and
`docs/glossary.md` explains every term.

## Why
Code without an explanation is a machine without a manual. The chapter is written for someone
learning, with analogies, so the platform can be understood and maintained by people who did
not build it.

## How to check it
Open `docs/guide/step-01-repo-workspace-and-link-to-project-0.md` and confirm it has 13
numbered sections. Mermaid diagrams render on GitHub.

## Risks and follow-ups
The setup guide's Project 0 addresses are assumptions until checked on the Mac.
