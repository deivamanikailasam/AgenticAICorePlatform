# Step 24 documentation: ADR 0035, guide chapter, glossary, PROGRESS

**Step:** 24 · **Commit:** docs(step-24): A2A chapter, ADR 0035, glossary and progress · **Date:** 2026-10-15

## What changed
- ADR 0035 records the A2A design and its alternatives:
  - pinned versions (a2a-sdk 1.2.1, grpcio 1.84.0);
  - signed cards;
  - the inbound gate (Keycloak, then OPA `a2a.inbound`);
  - one mount per agent;
  - remote agents as allowlisted tools with pinned keys;
  - replies as data;
  - push allowlist.
- New chapter `docs/guide/step-24-a2a-server-and-client.md` in the 13-section template.
- Glossary entries: A2A, Agent Card, signed Agent Card, pinned key, push notification.
- PROGRESS: Step 24 is done, with evidence. The next step is 25 (agent registry and
  discovery).

## Why
